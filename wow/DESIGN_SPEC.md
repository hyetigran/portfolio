# Design Specification

## Mood
Cinematic fantasy adventure at night. A quiet campsite overlooks an enormous illuminated civilization. The visual language should communicate craftsmanship, adventure, technical ambition and mystery.

## Foreground
Campfire at lower-left/center-left. Nearby: crates, barrels, chest, bench, lantern, bedroll/tent elements, banner pole, rocks, grasses and tools.

## Banner
American-inspired fantasy banner:
- recognizable red/white striping
- blue canton
- star motif
- medieval vertical hanging construction
- weathered fabric
- leather reinforcement
- ornate fantasy border
- metal/wood fittings
- slightly faded pigments
- no Horde insignia

## Midground
River, waterfalls, stone bridges, winding roads, houses, lanterns, towers, trees and fog layers.

## Background
Large mountain settlement with monumental castle, warm glowing windows, cliffs, waterfalls and a blue magical beacon.

## Sky
Large moon, stars, soft clouds and atmospheric haze.

## Lighting
Cool moonlight, warm campfire key light, warm emissive settlement windows and blue magical beacon. Keep overall lighting restrained.

## Animation
Slow, subtle always-on motion: fire flicker, embers, smoke, banner movement, water, particles/fireflies, clouds, foliage and occasional distant activity.

## UI
Secondary to the world. Use serif fantasy display typography, muted gold, translucent dark panels, thin borders and minimal icons. Avoid bright SaaS-style cards and opaque panels.

## Interaction
Eventually: hover subtly highlights world objects; click moves the camera to a location and opens a content panel. Navigation should feel like traveling through the world.

## Responsive
Desktop is primary. On mobile simplify scene quality if necessary, compact navigation, preserve key atmosphere, prioritize accessibility and respect reduced motion.
